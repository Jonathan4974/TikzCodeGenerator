The ST-GFN Dual-Stream Architecture. The model processes the negotiation tuple $\mathcal{N}$ via two parallel modalities. The Linguistic Stream (left) maps dialogue turns $T$ to semantic vectors $\mathbf{H}_{txt}$, while the Strategic Stream (right) models agent constraints as a directed graph $\mathcal{G}$. These streams are dynamically fused via a learned gate $z \in [0,1]$ to support prediction of both agreement occurrence and distributive outcomes in group decision and negotiation processes.
